A daily film guessing game.

LINEUP is free to play. Start with 24 films, ask up to five yes-or-no questions and identify one mystery film. There’s a new Daily challenge each day, plus unlimited Practice whenever you want another go.

How LINEUP works

Look over the posters. Ask about a year, genre, cast member, director or another film detail. Each answer removes films that don’t fit.

Use up to five questions, then choose a film and select Lock it in. You can make your final guess sooner if you’re ready.

Daily LINEUP or Practice?

Daily gives everyone the same lineup and mystery film, with one completed attempt per day in this browser. It changes at midnight UK time, following GMT and BST.

Practice makes a fresh lineup whenever you want. Play Practice between Daily puzzles; it doesn’t count towards account Daily statistics or streaks.

Why question choice matters

Five questions go quickly. Compare the films still in play before choosing what to ask. A detail shared by several films can be more useful than a fact about just one.

The opening examples show what you can ask. After that, use search or Browse questions to choose your own route through the lineup.

A few questions, answered.

Is LINEUP free?

Yes. Daily LINEUP and unlimited Practice are free to play.

How many questions do you get?

Up to five yes-or-no questions, followed by one final guess. You can lock in your answer before using all five.

Can you play more than once a day?

There’s one Daily puzzle each day. Switch to Practice for as many fresh lineups as you like.

When does the Daily puzzle change?

At midnight in the UK, using Europe/London time so the reset follows both GMT and BST.

Do you need an account?

No. Your Daily progress stays in this browser. Optional Google sign-in lets you save completed Daily results and see private statistics, history and winning streaks.
